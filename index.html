Our Core Statement of Faith

This is a suggested summary of foundational Christian beliefs. Please review it against the church’s officially approved statement.

1. The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our foundation for faith, teaching, and Christian living. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)

2. God

We believe in the one true God, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, according to historic Christian faith. (Matthew 28:19)

3. Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God, His incarnation, sinless life, death for our sins, bodily resurrection, ascension, and promised return. (1 Corinthians 15:3–4)

4. Salvation

We believe salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ, leading to repentance and a transformed life. (Ephesians 2:8–10)

5. The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit works in believers to bring spiritual renewal, godly character, empowerment for service, and growth in Christ. (Galatians 5:22–23)

6. The Church and the Great Commission

We believe the Church is called to worship God, make disciples, fellowship in faith, serve others, and proclaim the Gospel to all nations. (Matthew 28:19–20)

7. The Resurrection and Eternal Life

We believe in the resurrection of the dead, the return of Jesus Christ, and God’s promise of eternal life. (John 11:25–26)
